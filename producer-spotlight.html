The origins

Five ingredients. Thousands of years. One origin.

Papantla, Veracruz

Vanilla

Vanilla planifolia is native to Mexico’s Gulf Coast forests, where the Totonac people of Papantla have cultivated and cured it for generations.

Gulf Coast lowlands

Cacao

Residue in Olmec vessels at San Lorenzo shows cacao in use there between 1800 and 1000 BCE. The Maya later made it central to ritual and trade.

Yucatán Peninsula

Honey

The Maya kept native stingless melipona bees long before European honeybees arrived. Today the peninsula produces both melipona and mellifera honey.

Across Mexico

Heirloom maize

Maize was domesticated from teosinte in Mexico about 9,000 years ago. Heirloom varieties and nixtamalization remain the foundation of masa.

Veracruz highlands

Coffee

Coffee reached Córdoba, Veracruz, in the 1790s. Two centuries later, mountain growers carry a specialty tradition of their own.
