About us

Three brothers. One corridor.

Verdance began with three brothers who grew up in Mexico and a family business that has traded across the border since 2000. Working inside that trade, David, Ryan, and Andrew Landry watched promising relationships break down: too many middlemen taking a cut, quality that shifted from one shipment to the next, cultural gaps on both sides, and a system so complicated that producers and buyers often ended up working against each other. For years they operated in the middle themselves, trying to make the margins fairer. Verdance is what came next. Instead of taking a margin, we step out of it entirely. We qualify producers, keep quality and compliance consistent, and simplify how supply comes together, so buyers in the U.S., Europe, and the Middle East can buy directly from the source in relationships built to last.

The heritage

  • Honey, from the Maya tradition of melipona beekeeping.
  • Heirloom maize, first domesticated in Mexico.
